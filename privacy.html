Privacy Policy

Weexplainscience Poster. Last updated November 19, 2026.

Summary

Weexplainscience Poster is a private tool that uploads the Weexplainscience channel's own videos to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It does not collect data from viewers or from the public.

Information the tool handles

When the channel owner connects a platform account, the platform gives the tool an access token and basic account details, such as the account name and ID. The tool uses these only to upload the owner's videos and captions to that account and to check whether an upload succeeded.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Weexplainscience and are not shared with or sold to anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform they are being posted to.

What the tool does not do

It does not read messages, followers, viewers' data or other people's content, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not show advertising.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time in the connected-apps settings of Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) or TikTok. Deleting the tool's local data folder removes all stored tokens. To request deletion or ask a question, email the address below.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and does not collect information from them.
